Privacy & terms

Your data stays on this phone. Your vehicle, repair log and training examples are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. No accounts, cookies, ads, analytics or trackers.

Reminders you add to your calendar go to your phone's Calendar app. If you pick "Google Calendar", the reminder's title and date are sent to Google so it can create the event; nothing else is.

Delete it any time with the button below, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Guidance only. Car Doctor suggests likely causes from what you type. It can be wrong and is not a professional inspection or repair advice. If something feels unsafe (brakes, steering, overheating, fuel or exhaust smells), don't drive it and have a qualified mechanic check it. Use jack stands, chock the wheels, and let the engine cool before working on it. The app is free, provided as-is, and meant for adults; it isn't directed at children under 13.
